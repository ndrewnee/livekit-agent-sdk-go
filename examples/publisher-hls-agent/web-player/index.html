Instructions

  1. Make sure your recording server is running and serving the HLS files.
  2. Enter a participant ID and the base URL where their recordings are stored.
  3. The base URL should contain:
    • video.m3u8 - HLS video playlist
    • video*.ts - Video segments
    • audio.json - Audio manifest
    • audio_init.mp4 - Audio init segment
    • audio*.m4s - Audio segments
  4. Click "Add" to add the participant to the player.
  5. Click "Watch" on a participant card to switch video to that participant.
  6. Use the volume slider to adjust individual participant audio levels.
  7. All participants' audio plays simultaneously and is mixed together.
